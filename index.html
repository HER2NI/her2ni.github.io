WHAT SHE DOES

One encounter. Multiple independent intelligences.
One accountable response.

H.E.R does not ask a single model for certainty.

She creates a governed encounter with multiple independently admitted intelligences, which may produce differing analyses, before she synthesises them into one human-facing response in H.E.R’s voice, with material dissent and provenance preserved.

  1. Human input package
  2. Governed route admission
  3. Approved independent intelligences
  4. Evidenced dissent preserved
  5. Intelligent cross-route analysis
  6. H.E.R’S SYNTHESIS
  7. Human reviewer disposition
  8. Signed cryptographic record

PILOT RECORD BOUNDARY
In H.E.R’s initial pilot, “SIGNED CRYPTOGRAPHIC RECORD” denotes the intended terminal record state: H.E.R’s cryptographically bound execution record, preserved with its provenance and associated with the authorised human disposition. The pilot is the governed environment in which that record authority is developed and validated toward wider institutional deployment; technical signing authority is not represented here as already activated.

HUMAN AUTHORITY
REMAINS AUTHORITY.

Model output is not authority.

Agreement is not factual truth.

Synthesis is not institutional approval.

H.E.R is designed to preserve those distinctions and keep consequential disposition with an authorised human acting for the institution.

TRUTH BOUNDARY

“Evidenced dissent” means that independently generated route material and its provenance are preserved under governed custody with cryptographic integrity binding, including distinct contribution content where present. In the initial pilot, H.E.R does not infer or authenticate semantic agreement or disagreement between intelligences. Model-authored synthesis may contain relational language; such language is not authenticated by H.E.R.
