HOW H.E.R WORKS

One encounter. Multiple independent intelligences. One accountable response.

H.E.R does not ask one model to impersonate certainty.

It creates a governed encounter in which independently admitted intelligences may produce different analyses before one human-facing synthesis is produced, with material dissent and provenance preserved.

  1. Encounter
  2. Governed route admission
  3. Independent intelligences
  4. Freeze + compare
  5. Preserve material dissent
  6. Synthesis
  7. Human disposition
  8. Verifiable record

THE H.E.R RING™

A persistent identity for governed interaction.

The H.E.R Ring™ is the persistent visual identity of H.E.R.

Its state is designed to correspond to governed H.E.R interaction conditions rather than random animation.

H.E.R may use perceptible state to communicate process conditions such as execution, availability, material difference, synthesis and review while preserving truthful failure and human authority.

THE HUMAN REMAINS THE AUTHORITY.

Model output is not authority.

Agreement is not factual truth.

Synthesis is not institutional approval.

H.E.R is designed to preserve those distinctions and keep consequential disposition with an authorised human or institution.
