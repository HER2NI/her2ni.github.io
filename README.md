## H.E.R™

H.E.R™ is being developed as an interaction, authority and provenance layer
between humans and multiple artificial intelligences.

Its architectural direction includes:

- policy-bounded, task-specific route selection;
- independent execution of admitted intelligences;
- governed information exposure and route projection;
- one synthesised human-facing response;
- explicit representation of route outcomes and unavailable evidence;
- interaction provenance and reconstructable records;
- explicit human review and disposition;
- provider-neutral operation; and
- human authority over consequential action.

H.E.R™ does not treat model output as institutional authority and does not
treat agreement between models as factual truth.

The system remains under active development. Public website material should
not be read as a claim of government accreditation, production readiness or
deployment authority.

## HER2NI® Research

HER2NI® began as research into structural properties of human–AI interaction.

That research includes model-agnostic interaction telemetry, coherence and
trajectory concepts, including the historical Cₛ, Sₛ, Hₛ and Hₛ(t)
constructs, together with later work on structural telemetry, interaction
topology, provenance, route governance and multi-intelligence systems.

The research lineage is preserved separately from current H.E.R™ product

## Research and Safety Boundaries

HER2NI® research does not, merely by measuring interaction structure:

- diagnose medical or psychological conditions;
- infer private mental states;
- determine factual truth;
- determine legal conclusions;
- create institutional decision authority; or
- autonomously execute consequential actions.

Experimental metrics and research artifacts should be interpreted according
to the limitations stated in their individual specifications and publications.
